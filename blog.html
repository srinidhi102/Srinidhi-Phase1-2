I'm interested to research more on Cyber Risk Quantification and gain work experience in that industry.

I recently attended The Hybrid event: Cyber Risk Quantification, where I had the pleasure of meeting Tim Fletcher and James Hanbury and gaining valuable insights from their experience at KPMG into quantitative methods and impact modeling are transforming cyber risk management. One of the most engaging parts of the event was learning about the decomposition method, which helps break down complex cyber scenarios into measurable components. I also found it impressive to explore how an insights database codified from extensive public domain data, supports data-driven risk assessment. A key takeaway for me was the importance of avoiding over-inflation of risk by minimising overlap between scenarios which can ensure accuracy of quanitification models. As a Bsc Computer Science student at Queen Mary University of London, this experience has strengthened my ambition to explore more opportunities in cyber security, particularly in CRQ and security analytics.
